LazyDogTemplates is a digital design marketplace and creative technology platform built for people who want to create, edit, transform, and present professional visual content without being locked into a single design tool.

We started with a simple idea: digital templates should be more than files you download and forget.

Today, LazyDog brings templates, AI-powered creation, editing, conversion, and presentation workflows together in one ecosystem.

From Templates to a Complete Creative Workflow

Our platform supports a wide range of digital products, including presentation decks, media kits, website kits, resumes and CVs, invoices, and other professional design resources.

But the marketplace is only one part of what we are building.

LazyDog's editor is designed to bridge different creative workflows, including PowerPoint, Canva, and Figma. It can work with complex design assets and transform otherwise difficult-to-edit content into editable design elements.

Our goal is simple: give creators and buyers more freedom over the designs they work with.

AI-Powered Creation

With our Hexa AI system, users can go beyond editing an existing template.

They can describe the kind of design or presentation they need, define their requirements, choose a visual direction, and generate a completely new design.

Our metadata-driven design system helps maintain visual consistency across large presentations while giving users control over style, audience, tone, colors, backgrounds, imagery, and other design characteristics.

Whether you need a few slides or a large presentation, LazyDog is designed to turn your requirements into a finished visual product.

Turning Flat Designs Into Editable Designs

One of the areas we continue to develop is intelligent design reconstruction.

Our editor can analyse complex visual content and break it into individual editable elements. This allows designs that might otherwise behave like a single flat image or document to become much more useful inside an editable workflow.

We are continuing to expand this technology across different types of visual content, including 2D and 3D designs.

Built for Creators and Buyers

LazyDogTemplates is designed for both sides of the creative marketplace.

For buyers:

  • Find professional designs, customize them, generate new content, and take your work into the tools and formats you already use.

For contributors:

  • Submit original digital products, reach new audiences, and become part of a growing creative ecosystem.

Every submitted product remains subject to review and approval. We believe quality and usefulness matter more than simply filling a marketplace with files.

Our Vision

We believe the future of digital design is not about choosing between a marketplace, an editor, an AI tool, or a presentation platform.

It is about bringing those experiences together.

Our long-term vision is to build a creative ecosystem where a user can:

Discover → Create → Edit → Transform → Present → Share

all within one connected workflow.

And as LazyDog grows, we want creators and digital marketplaces to have more opportunities to reach audiences beyond a single platform.

We're Just Getting Started

LazyDogTemplates is continuously evolving.

New design categories, editing capabilities, AI workflows, file-conversion technologies, and creator tools are being developed as we move forward.

We are building LazyDog for creators who want their work to go further — and for buyers who want more from the designs they purchase.
