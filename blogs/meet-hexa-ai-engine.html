This is the big one. Hexa is LazyDog's own AI engine — built in-house and wired straight into the site. It's in beta and under active development, so it's still growing and improving day by day. And it isn't a chatbot bolted on the side; it's an action engine. Ask it a question and it answers. Ask it to find, design, or build, and it actually does. One box, one intelligence, real work coming out the other side.

AI operating system for presentations
Welcome to Hexa
Describe any presentation, media kit, or pitch deck. Hexa finds, builds, edits, and prepares it for you.

One Box, Three Modes

Everything Hexa does starts from a single prompt box with three ways to work: Chat, Create Design, and Create Presentation. You pick the mode, type what you want in plain language, and Hexa takes it from there. Same place, different jobs — no tools to juggle, no pages to hunt through.

It Chats — and It Acts

In Chat mode you can ask Hexa anything — about templates, pricing, or just say hi. But it doesn't stop at conversation. Describe what you're looking for and it will search the library for you, surfacing the right designs from a plain-language request. That's the whole idea: it's not just a chatbot, it's an action bot. It answers you, and then it goes and does the thing.

It Makes Designs for You

Switch to Create Design and Hexa becomes a designer. Describe the design you're picturing — or set a few preferences like content type, number of slides, background, accent colour, industry, and image weight — then press Generate. Hexa builds you a brand-new design on the spot. No blank canvas, no manual layout: you describe it, it designs it.

It Builds Presentations for You

Switch to Create Presentation and Hexa becomes your deck builder. Bring your content — paste it in or drop a file — and choose a design, then hit Prepare my deck. Hexa pours your words into the design and hands you a finished presentation. It even checks that your content fits the design and tells you if it needs a little more room, so nothing ends up crammed.

Some Templates, Made by Hexa

Under Development

This isn't just talk. The showcase on the page is a set of templates Hexa produced itself — media kits, pitch decks, keynotes, and more, each finished and on-brand. It's an early look: this capability is still under development and keeps getting sharper as Hexa grows.

What Hexa Is, In One Line

Hexa turns "I need a deck, a design, or an answer" into the thing itself.

Chat, search, design, build — one assistant that acts, not just talks. It's LazyDog's own engine, built into the site, and it's only just getting started. Everything you've seen across these updates — fonts and colours, the design maker, instant matching, the fill flow — Hexa is the intelligence tying them together.
