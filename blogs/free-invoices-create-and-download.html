Good news — your invoices are on us. LazyDogTemplates now has a completely free invoice generator: pick a style, fill in your details, and download a clean, professional invoice in minutes. No sign-up, no cost.

Four Steps, Then Send

Choose your type, pick your industry, select a design, then fill and download. That's the whole flow — no account to create, nothing to install. You can have a finished invoice ready to send before a paid tool would finish loading.

Everything a Real Invoice Needs
