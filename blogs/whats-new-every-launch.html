Every time we ship something new, it lands here first. What's New is the site's running launch reel — a scrollable, keynote-style page that introduces LazyDog Templates and shows what's just been added. It's never "finished": it keeps updating as the library and the features grow.

A Keynote, Not a Changelog

Most sites bury updates in a dry list of version notes. We didn't want that. What's New is built like a keynote — it opens on a title moment, animates as you scroll, and walks you through the highlights the way a good presentation would. The first thing you meet is the library itself.

Not Just Slides — A Whole Toolkit

From there it lays out what's actually inside, one pillar at a time. It's a quick, honest summary of what the library gives you — and where new work keeps landing.

It Keeps Updating

The reason to check back is simple: this is where every launch shows up. As we roll out new features — the kind you've seen in recent updates, from site-wide fonts and colours to voice, the design maker, and instant matching — What's New is where they're announced and shown off. The page grows with the product, so it always reflects the latest version of LazyDog.

Why Start Here

For a first-time visitor, What's New is the fastest way to grasp the whole picture — what the library is, what's inside, and why it's worth your time — without digging through the store. For someone coming back, it's the quickest way to see what's changed since last time.

Think of it as the site's headline reel: open it whenever you want to know what's newest, then dive into whatever catches your eye.
