Media Kits is the newest section on LazyDogTemplates — and just like Pitch Decks, we're opening it small and on purpose.

What you'll find here is a focused set of press-ready media kit samples — brand story, leadership bios, logo lockups, and fact sheets — not an endless catalog you have to dig through to find something usable.

The Same Philosophy — Quality First

It would be easy to fill this section with dozens of kits overnight. It's much harder to make sure each one is genuinely useful — the kind of kit a journalist or partner can actually work from without rebuilding it themselves.

We'd rather ship a few that are right than a pile that's just noise. The count matters far less than whether the kit does its job the moment someone opens it.

What a Good Media Kit Actually Needs

A media kit isn't just a logo and a paragraph. The ones worth using pull the whole story together in one place — who you are, what you stand for, the people behind it, the facts a reporter needs, and clean, high-res assets ready to drop straight into an article.

Everything consistent, on-brand, and easy to hand off. That's the bar we're building every sample in this section to meet.

What's Coming Next

More kits, more industries, and the same smart search we're building across the whole site — so when you need a media kit for a specific moment, you land on the right one fast instead of scrolling.

This is the starting point for Media Kits, not the finish line. Like everything here, it grows once the foundation is right.
