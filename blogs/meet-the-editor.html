Meet the Editor — Design Anything, Right in the Browser

LD LazyDog Team  ·  23 Jun 2026
LazyDogTemplates  |  LazyDog Studios
← Back to Journal

The most powerful piece of LazyDog is now open: a full design editor, built right into the site. It's a complete studio in your browser — the familiar power of a PowerPoint-style ribbon meets the ease of a Canva-style toolkit — where you can open, edit, and finish any deck without another app. It's in beta and under active development, and this is just the first look.

The Big One
Your slides stay consistent — everywhere
Bring a deck into the editor and it holds every element of your slides together, so the file opens cleanly in Canva, Google Slides, PowerPoint, or Keynote — without the layout breaking. No shifted text, no jumbled shapes, no "why does it look different over here?" What you designed is what everyone sees, wherever they open it.

The standout feature — cross-app consistency, so your work never falls apart in transit.

What Can You Design in the Editor?

The editor is built for slide-based content — pitch decks, media kits, presentations, look books, brand decks, and keynote presentations. You can start from any LazyDog template and customise it fully, upload your own PPTX file and edit it in the browser, or build from scratch using the templates, elements, icons, illustrations, and grids in the left panel.

Every element is editable: text, images, shapes, colours, layouts, and backgrounds. The Animate and Effects panels add motion and visual polish when you want them. When you're done, you download a file that opens cleanly in any app your client or colleague uses — no "it looks different on my machine" conversations.

A Ribbon You Already Know

Across the top sits a familiar ribbon — Home, Arrange, Design, View, Review, Present — organised exactly the way office software is. Clipboard tools, fonts and sizes, WordArt, bold / italic / underline, text colour and highlight, alignment, bullets and numbering, spacing, and select / find. If you've ever built a slide before, there's nothing new to learn.

A Canva-Style Toolkit Down the Side

The left rail is where the creativity lives: Templates, Elements, Icons, Illustrations, Stickers, Frames, Grids, Brand, Fonts, Layers, Pages, Comments — plus Animate and Effects for motion and polish. Everything you need to build or restyle a slide is a single click away, no hunting through menus.

How It Compares to Canva and PowerPoint

Canva is excellent for design, but its exported files often break when opened in PowerPoint or Google Slides — fonts substitute, shapes shift, spacing changes. PowerPoint is powerful but runs only on desktop, requires a licence, and doesn't have the fluid creative toolkit that browser-based tools do. The LazyDog editor sits between them: it has Canva's creative ease and PowerPoint's familiarity, but with cross-app consistency built in from the start so the file you export is the file everyone sees.

For freelancers who work with clients on different platforms, that last point matters most. You finish a deck in the editor, send the PPTX, and the client opens it in Google Slides exactly as you designed it. No revision round because the title font changed on their machine.

Open, Edit, Present, Export

Upload a deck to bring it in, edit it as freely as you like, rehearse it in Present mode, then Download when you're done. It's round-trip friendly, so the file you take out stays as clean as the one you brought in.
