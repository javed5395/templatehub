New on the Home Page

Jump Straight to Your Favourite Tools

The home page just got five handy new cards — Figma, Canva, Google Slides, PowerPoint, and Keynote. They're quick-launch shortcuts: tap one and that app opens in your browser, so you can jump straight into the tool you like best without hunting for the link.

Five Tools, One Tap Away

Each card is a direct door to a major design or presentation app. Click it and the app opens in a new tab, ready for you to work:

Figma
Modern UI & design templates — opens Figma in your browser.
Canva
Simple drag-and-drop design — opens Canva in your browser.
Google Slides
Free presentations in the browser — opens Google Slides.
PowerPoint
Professional slide decks — opens PowerPoint on the web.
Keynote
Apple's presentation app — opens Keynote.

You Sign In With Your Own Account

When you tap a card, it takes you to that platform — Figma, Canva, Google, Microsoft, or Apple. To actually start working there, you'll sign in with your own account for that service: your Google login for Slides, your Microsoft account for PowerPoint, your Apple ID for Keynote, and so on. LazyDog simply opens the door — your own login is what lets you in and keeps your work in your account.

Why We Put Them Here

It's all about making your workflow faster and smoother. Instead of leaving the site, opening a new tab, and typing a URL, you get one-tap access to the exact tool you want — right where you're already browsing templates.

So you can grab a LazyDog template, then hop straight into Figma, Canva, Slides, PowerPoint, or Keynote to build with it. Browse here, design there — whichever app you prefer is always a click away, and you never lose your place.

How to Use Them

Open the main page, scroll to the row of cards just below the hero, click the tool you want to use, sign in with your account for that service, and you're off. That's the whole thing — a shortcut built to save you a few clicks every single time.
