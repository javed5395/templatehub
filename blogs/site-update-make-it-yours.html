Fonts — Change the Typeface Everywhere

Open Features → Font and pick a typeface. The moment you choose, every heading and every paragraph across the whole site switches to it — instantly, and everywhere at once. Not a preview on one page: the entire site adopts your font as you browse.

It's the fastest way to make the site feel like yours. Prefer something crisp and modern, or warm and editorial? Pick it, and the whole experience re-types itself around your taste.

And it sticks. Your choice follows you from page to page as you move around the site, so you're not re-picking it every time — the whole place simply reads the way you set it. Nothing is downloaded and nothing breaks; the layout stays exactly as designed, just in the typeface you chose.

Colour — Recolour the Whole Site

Next to fonts sits the colour picker. Its first tab, Theme, controls the site's colours. Drag across the gradient, slide the hue bar, or type an exact value as HEX or R/G/B — then press Apply, and the site's theme colour updates everywhere at once. Changed your mind? Reset puts it back to default.

Because you can type an exact HEX or R/G/B value, this isn't just a rough tint — you can match a precise brand colour down to the number. Set it once and the accent runs consistently across buttons, highlights, and links everywhere on the site.

Font Colour — A Separate Tab, Same Picker

The picker's second tab, Fonts, controls your text colour. Switch to it and choose a colour exactly the same way — gradient, hue, or a precise HEX/RGB value. Apply it, and the site's text instantly takes on that colour. It sits right beside the Theme tab, so you can set your background palette and your text colour without ever leaving the picker.

Keeping the two in one place makes it easy to get the pairing right — a theme colour you love and a text colour that stays crisp and readable against it, dialled in together rather than guessed separately.

Voice — Talk Instead of Type

Tired of writing? Tap Voice to open the mic, then just speak. The assistant listens and acts on what you say — it turns your spoken words into actions on the site, so you can get things done hands-free while you sit back.

How it works is simple: click the mic to start, say what you want in plain language, and it responds. When you're done, tap it off. No typing, no menus to hunt through — you talk, it works.

It's built for the moments when your hands are busy or you just don't feel like typing — ask it to move around the site, find what you're after, or kick off an action, all by speaking. Say it once, and it carries out the command for you.

Language — Read the Site in Your Own Language

Finally, the language tab. Click it and pick from a long list of languages — the entire site converts to your choice on the spot, top to bottom. English, Español, العربية, 日本語, Deutsch, Français, Italiano, اردو, 한국어 and many more are all one tap away, with a "more" option opening the full list of 25-plus languages.

It handles right-to-left languages properly too — pick العربية, اردو, or فارسی and the site lays itself out in the correct direction, not just translated word-for-word. Wherever you're reading from, the site meets you in your own language.

All In One Place

Everything here lives in the top bar. Font, Colour, and Voice sit under the Features menu; the font-colour control is the second tab of the same colour picker; and the language switch is the globe beside it. Nothing to set up, nothing to learn — open it, choose, and the site responds.

Together, these updates do one thing: they let you shape the site around how you like to work. Your fonts, your colours, your text colour, your voice, your language — all live now, all yours.
