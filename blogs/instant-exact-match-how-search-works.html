The hardest part of any template marketplace was never the templates — it's finding the right one. Instant & Exact Match fixes that. Instead of scrolling through pages, you describe what you need, and the closest kits come to you — ranked, so the best fit is right at the top.

You can talk to it in plain language, narrow things down with a few filters, or do both at once. Either way, it reads the qualities that actually matter for a kit and lines up the closest matches first.

Two Ways to Search

There's no single "right" way to use it. Some people think in sentences, some in filters — it accepts both, and they work together.

Say It in Plain Words

The simplest way in is to just type what you're making, the way you'd say it out loud: "a bold 12-slide pitch deck for investors," or "an elegant fashion media kit for press." You don't have to get it all out in one go — add one detail at a time, and the search keeps refining as you talk.

It picks up the practical things you mention — whether it's a pitch deck or a media kit, roughly how many slides or pages you want, the shape you need it in — and folds them straight into the results without you touching a single menu.

Or Narrow With Filters

Prefer to point and click? The filter panel covers the same ground with dropdowns. Set the Industry — finance, retail, real estate, fashion, food, education, travel, and more. Choose a Style or tone — professional, confident, bold, elegant, editorial, playful. Pick the Audience you're speaking to — investors, clients, press, partners, recruiters. And name the Purpose — pitching, a launch, press distribution, a partnership, a job application, a portfolio.

Every filter you set is another signal, and you can mix them with your typed description freely. Words and filters aren't two separate tools here — they shape the same result.

Ranked by Match, Not Guesswork

Results don't just appear in a random pile. Each kit comes back with a match score, shown as a small gauge, so you can see at a glance how close it is to what you asked for. The strongest fit sits at the top, with the runners-up right behind it.

What makes the ranking fair is that every detail counts evenly. The right slide count matters as much as the right colour, which matters as much as the right industry — so the top result is genuinely the best all-round fit, not just the newest upload or the one with the flashiest cover.

From Match to Finished Kit

Finding the kit is only half of it. Once the right one is at the top, you move straight into the "prepare it for me" flow — the kit becomes your starting point, ready to be filled with your own content. Search isn't the finish line; it's the on-ramp to a deck that's already yours.

Getting the Best Results

A few small habits make the search noticeably sharper. Be specific — "16-slide investor pitch, confident tone" beats "a good deck." Add details gradually and watch the matches tighten. Mix a typed sentence with a filter or two when you want to be precise. And if the results feel too narrow, clear a filter to widen the field again.

That's the whole idea: describe what you need in your own words, let the ranking do the sorting, and spend your time choosing — not scrolling.
