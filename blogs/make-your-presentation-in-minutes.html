Step 1 · Where Your Content Goes

On the left, you hand over your words. Paste them straight into the "Describe your content" box — headings and text for the slides, in whatever order you have them. If it already lives in a document, use "Or load a file" instead and drop in a plain-text or Word file (.txt or .docx). Either way, you're providing the raw material, not laying out slides.

Step 2 · Where Your Design Goes

On the right, you choose the look. Drag the design you want filled into the "Drag your design" box — a deck (.pptx) that becomes the shell your content is built into. That's how the finished deck ends up carrying the style you picked, wrapped around the words you brought.

Step 3 · Prepare My Deck — and the Fit Check

With content on one side and a design on the other, press Prepare my deck. Before anything is built, the system looks at your content, then at the design, and tells you plainly how the two line up — so you never end up with slides that overflow or sit half-empty.

🟢 Your content fits this design perfectly.
🟡 Fits — your text is rich, so a couple of slides will be added to give it room.
🔴 Your content is heavier than this design holds — here's how many extra slides it needs.

When Your Content Is Heavy, It Adds Slides — With Your Approval

This is the part we designed carefully. If your content is too much for the design you chose, the system doesn't silently squeeze it in or quietly reshape your deck. It tells you your content is heavy, shows you exactly how many extra slides are needed, and waits for your go-ahead. Only when you approve does it add those slides and fill them with your content — so the final length is always your decision.

And if a design is simply too small for everything you want to say, it points you to other designs that can comfortably hold it, so you can switch rather than trim your message.

Prefer a Faster Start?

If you don't have content ready, the other two routes get you moving just as quickly. Pick a pre-made design and make it yours, or describe a look and have a brand-new design generated for you on the site. Same destination — a finished deck — from whichever starting point suits the moment.

Minutes, Not Hours

However you begin, the goal is the same: skip the slow part and land on slides you can actually use. The fill route and the design generator are in beta and under active testing, so you may find them locked or evolving from visit to visit — that's on purpose while we get them right. More designs and wider access are on the way.
