There's a new page live on the site — and this one isn't a feature, it's a preview. The Next Chapter is a cinematic teaser for what's coming next: three new worlds opening inside LazyDog, each built for a different kind of person. Here's what it reveals.

LazyDog Studios presents
The Next Chapter
Three new worlds are opening inside LazyDog.

The opening frame — "LazyDog Studios presents: The Next Chapter."

It opens the way a film does — a black screen, a single line: "What happens when creators stop working alone?" — and an answer: a platform becomes an ecosystem. Then it names the three worlds about to open.

The First World — Open Affiliate

The first world is a new home for marketers — but not as employees or salespeople. As partners. It's framed for people who understand businesses, who bring opportunities, and who create connections. The teaser is clear about the spirit of it: this is a place to partner with LazyDog, not to be hired by it. If your strength is opening doors and building relationships, this world is being built for you.

The Second World — The Contributor Program

The second world is about collective intelligence — opening the studio's craft up to a wider circle of makers who want to contribute to the library itself. The teaser spells out exactly who it has in mind:

UI DesignersUX DesignersDevelopers Brand SpecialistsResearchersPresentation Designers Motion ArtistsCreative Thinkers

Who Can Join — a wide door for designers, developers, and creative minds.

The Third World — Edit Your Template

The third world is for creators, and it's about creation without friction. The promise is simple: the template becomes yours — no waiting, no complexity. It points toward a new generation of templates you can open, edit, and make your own on the spot, so the distance between "I like this" and "this is mine" all but disappears.

One Ecosystem

Woven together, the three worlds are the point: LazyDog growing from a platform into an ecosystem, with a place for the people who market it, the people who build for it, and the people who create with it. The teaser even names why it's being built — the belief that the whole becomes far more than the sum of templates once marketers, contributors, and creators all have a home in it.

None of these are open just yet — that's the whole point of the page. It's a first glimpse, a title card for what's ahead, ending on a single promise.
