LazyDogTemplates has always been about templates first. But building templates meant building tools alongside them — an editor to open them in, a crawler to keep the site healthy, small engines that solve one problem well. Those tools used to live scattered across the site. Today they get a proper home: a new Tools section, with every app LazyDog Studios builds in one place.

What's in it today

LazyDog Editor — Live

The design editor already powering every template on this site is the first app listed. Open it straight from your browser, or grab the Windows app from the Microsoft Store.

SEO Fix Engine — Coming soon

The same crawler and fix-engine we use to keep this site healthy, packaged as a tool anyone can point at their own website.

That is the whole idea: things we build for ourselves, tidied up enough to hand to you. Some will be free, some will be paid, some one-time and some subscription — we will be upfront about which is which as each one ships.

Why now

The editor has quietly grown from "a way to edit our own templates" into something people use on its own. It made sense to give it — and whatever comes after it — a page of its own instead of leaving it buried in a footer link.
