Two Ways to Make a Design

You're in charge of how much you say. Write a free-form description, set the preference fields, or do both together — whatever's quickest for you. Fill in as little or as much as you like; anything you leave open simply gives the designer room to choose for you.

Just Describe It

The fastest route is the description box. Write your design the way you'd explain it to a designer — for example: "luxury fashion media kit, 15 slides, black background, gold accents, editorial layout, inspired by the Aurora Skincare kit, 5 mockup slides." Mention the pieces that matter to you and skip the rest. The more specific you are, the closer the first result lands.

Or Set the Preferences

Prefer to be precise? The preferences panel lets you pin down each choice with a field. Here's what each one does and how to fill it:

Content Type
What you're making (pitch deck, media kit, and so on). Leave it on "Any" to let the designer decide, or pick one to lock it.
No. of Slides
How many slides you want. Type a number, e.g. 15.
Background
The base look, like dark or light. Choose one, or leave "Any".
Accent Colour
Your highlight colour (gold, blue, and the like) used for emphasis. Pick one or leave it open.
Industry / Topic
Your field, such as fashion, finance, or food, so the look suits your world.
Image Weight
How image-heavy versus text-heavy the design should feel: balanced, more images, or more text.
Mock-up Slides
How many product or device mock-up slides to include. Give a number or a share, e.g. 5, or 20%.
Use a Past Design's Look
Borrow the look of a design you've seen before, e.g. "design 3 background".
Inspired by / Base Layout On
Name a design or brand to base the layout on, so the result starts from a direction you already like.

Then Hit "Make My Design"

Once your description or fields are set, press Make my design and the design is built to match. Because every field is optional, you can go from a one-line description to a fully-specified brief — and get a starting point either way.

It's in Beta — and We're Testing

This is an early release, so access is limited while we run trials and refine how it behaves — you may find it locked or changing from visit to visit. That's deliberate: we'd rather get it right than rush it.

More is on the way — wider access, more choices in every field, and an even smoother path from a few words to a finished design. This is your first look at making a LazyDog design entirely your own.
