Set 05 · Application Letters · Cover Letters

The application letter, as asked for here.

Most job advertisements in Pakistan, the Philippines, India and the Gulf ask for an "application letter", not a cover letter. They are not the same document, and sending the wrong one is a small but real disadvantage. An application letter is shorter, more formal, states the post explicitly, and lists what is enclosed. Three samples: a school post, a hospital post, and a walk-in advertisement.

Sample OnePrivate school · Newspaper advertisement

A private school advertisement, answered in the expected form: post named, qualification stated, documents listed.

Ayesha Malik
ayesha.malik@email.com  ·  +92 333 2218 907  ·  House 14, Street 6, Satellite Town, Rawalpindi
12 August 2026
The Principal Roots Millennium School Westridge Campus Rawalpindi
Application for the post of English Teacher (Middle Section)
Respected Sir/Madam,

With reference to your advertisement published in the daily Jang on 8 August 2026, I wish to apply for the post of English Teacher for the middle section.

I have completed my B.Ed from the University of Education, Rawalpindi in 2025 with a 3.5 CGPA, preceded by a BA in English Literature from Fatima Jinnah Women University. I have completed 320 hours of supervised teaching practice across two schools and have served four months as a substitute teacher for Grades 6 to 8 at this same campus, where I managed classes of up to 32 students and marked all assessments on schedule.

I hold current child protection and safeguarding certification and am available to join immediately.

I shall be thankful if I am granted an opportunity for an interview and a demonstration lesson. My documents are enclosed for your kind consideration.

Yours faithfully,
Ayesha Malik
Enclosed: (1) Curriculum vitae (2) B.Ed degree and transcript (3) BA degree (4) Teaching practice certificate (5) Safeguarding certificate (6) CNIC copy (7) Two photographs
Why this one works
  • The newspaper and date are quoted. Schools run several advertisements at once, and this is how the application is routed to the right file.
  • The substitute work at the same campus is named. Prior familiarity with the school is the strongest thing in the letter and it sits in the main paragraph, not at the end.
  • A demonstration lesson is requested. Teaching interviews almost always include one, and asking for it signals confidence and familiarity with the process.
  • Enclosures are numbered. Seven items. Schools reject incomplete files administratively, without explanation.
  • Full postal address is given. Correct convention here, unlike a Western application where a city is enough.
Sample TwoPublic hospital · Nursing post

A public hospital nursing post. Registration number, category and experience in years are what the file is assessed on.

Fatima Noor
fatima.noor@email.com  ·  +92 300 1234 567  ·  Flat 4-B, Block 13, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Karachi  ·  PNC Reg. 44192
12 August 2026
The Medical Superintendent Jinnah Postgraduate Medical Centre Rafiqui Shaheed Road Karachi
Application for the post of Charge Nurse (BPS-17) — Advertisement dated 2 August 2026
Respected Sir,

In response to the advertisement published on 2 August 2026 for the post of Charge Nurse (BPS-17), I submit my application for your kind consideration.

I am a registered nurse with the Pakistan Nursing Council (Registration No. 44192, valid to 2029) and hold a BSc in Nursing from Dow University of Health Sciences. I have six years of post-registration experience, presently serving as Senior Staff Nurse at Aga Khan University Hospital, where I manage a twelve-patient medical-surgical ward across rotating shifts and have precepted eight junior nurses.

During my present service I introduced a medication double-check protocol which reduced dispensing errors by thirty percent, and I have maintained ninety-eight percent documentation compliance across six quarterly audits. I hold current BLS and ACLS certification from the American Heart Association.

All required documents are enclosed. I shall be grateful for the opportunity to appear before the selection committee.

Yours faithfully,
Fatima Noor
Enclosed: (1) Curriculum vitae (2) PNC registration certificate (3) BSN degree and transcript (4) Experience certificates (5) BLS/ACLS certificates (6) CNIC copy (7) Domicile certificate (8) Three photographs
Why this one works
  • Registration number appears twice. In the letterhead and in the body with its validity. Clinical files are checked for it before anything else.
  • Experience is stated as post-registration years. Public sector eligibility is defined that way, so it must be phrased that way.
  • Numbers appear even in the formal register. Thirty percent, ninety-eight percent, twelve patients — written out in words to suit the formality without losing the information.
  • Domicile is included. Provincial quota rules apply to public sector recruitment and an omitted domicile certificate voids the application.
Sample ThreePrivate company · Carried by hand

A walk-in interview advertisement. The letter will be handed over in person, read in under a minute, and possibly acted on the same day.

Waleed Anjum
waleed.anjum@email.com  ·  +92 302 7719 338  ·  Gujranwala, Pakistan
12 August 2026
The Sales Manager Ittefaq Building Supplies (Pvt) Ltd GT Road, Gujranwala
Application for the post of Territory Sales Officer
Respected Sir,

With reference to your walk-in interview advertisement dated 10 August 2026, I present myself for the post of Territory Sales Officer.

I ran my own retail hardware business for seven years, growing annual turnover from PKR 4 million to PKR 26 million and managing 140 active trade accounts. I negotiated terms directly with twelve distributors and improved average margin by six points. Prior to that I worked two years as a sales assistant in this same trade, and I know the product range and the distributor structure of this district well.

I hold a B.Com from the University of Gujrat and have a motorcycle and valid licence for territory work.

I am available to join immediately and have brought all documents with me.

Yours faithfully,
Waleed Anjum
Enclosed: (1) Curriculum vitae (2) B.Com degree (3) CNIC copy (4) Two photographs (5) Driving licence copy
Why this one works
  • "I present myself" matches the format. A walk-in is an in-person format and the letter acknowledges it rather than pretending to be posted.
  • Local knowledge is claimed specifically. "The product range and the distributor structure of this district" is worth more to a territory role than any general sales claim.
  • The motorcycle is mentioned. For field sales this is a practical condition of the job, and stating it removes a question.
  • "Brought all documents with me" closes the loop. On a walk-in day this means the candidate can be processed immediately, and some are.

Application letter vs cover letter

The two are treated as synonyms in a lot of online advice written for a Western audience. In practice, in Pakistan and much of South Asia and the Gulf, they are different documents and employers expect the local one.

  • The application letter is formal by default. "Respected Sir/Madam", "Yours faithfully", no contractions, no conversational opening.
  • It names the post and the advertisement explicitly in a subject line, including the newspaper or portal and the date.
  • It states qualifications directly — degree, institution, year, division or CGPA.
  • It lists enclosures, numbered.
  • It is shorter, typically three or four short paragraphs.
  • It closes with a request to be granted an interview.

A Western-style cover letter opening with a striking sentence about your achievements is not wrong, exactly — but against fifty conventional letters it reads as unfamiliar with the process, and against an assessment committee that costs more than it gains.

Formal still allows numbers

The commonest weakness in application letters is that formality is used as a substitute for content. Three paragraphs of impeccable courtesy that say nothing measurable.

Every sample on this page keeps the formal register and still carries figures — ward size, error reduction, turnover growth, teaching hours, account numbers. The convention governs the grammar and the courtesy; it does not require you to be vague.

A useful habit: after writing the letter, count the numbers in it. If there are none, the letter is doing nothing your qualifications certificate does not already do.

The enclosure list

Number the enclosures and list them at the foot of the letter. This is not a formality — it is how the receiving clerk checks the file is complete, and an incomplete file is set aside before anyone assesses merit.

Standard list for most local applications: curriculum vitae, degree certificates and transcripts, experience certificates, professional registration, CNIC copy, domicile certificate for public sector posts, and photographs.

Two things that quietly reject files: photocopies where the advertisement said attested, and a missing domicile certificate on a public sector application where provincial quota rules apply. Read the advertisement twice and supply exactly what it lists.
