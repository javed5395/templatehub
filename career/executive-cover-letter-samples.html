Executive letter, peer to peer.

A senior letter is not an application, it is the opening of a negotiation between two people of comparable standing. It is written to a chairman, a founder or a search partner — never to an HR inbox — and the register changes completely. Three samples: a response to a search consultant, a direct approach to a chairman, and a letter accompanying a board application.

Sample OneRetained search · CEO shortlist

A headhunter is not your reader — their client is. The letter has to give the consultant something they can repeat in a meeting you will not attend.

Shahid Chaudhry
s.chaudhry@executive.com  ·  +92 300 4471 200  ·  Karachi & Dubai
12 August 2026
Ms. Ayesha Sethi Partner, Industrial Practice Korn Ferry Pakistan Karachi
Chief Financial Officer — listed manufacturing group (your reference KF/2026/118)
Dear Ayesha,

Thank you for the brief. I am interested, and I think the position is closer to my last two roles than the specification suggests.

The brief describes a group that has grown quickly, carries more debt than the board is comfortable with, and has a finance function that cannot close in under three weeks. I have taken exactly that combination once before. At Crescent Steel we lifted gross margin from 18.2% to 27.6%, raised PKR 14 billion while reducing net gearing from 2.1× to 0.8×, and got the board pack out five working days after month end. The margin and the gearing moved in the same period, which is the part I would want to discuss.

On the two areas the specification flags as essential: I have taken a group through IPO, and I led the finance workstream on a $258 million acquisition and its fourteen-month integration.

What I would want to understand before going further is how much of the current position is inherited and how much is structural, and what the chairman believes the answer is. I am available to speak this week or next.

Kind regards,
Shahid Chaudhry
Enclosed: curriculum vitae (2 pages)
Why this one works
  • It reads the brief back. Three sentences summarising the client's actual problem prove the specification was understood rather than skimmed.
  • The consultant is given a repeatable line. "Margin and gearing moved in the same period" is the sentence they will use when presenting the shortlist.
  • Essential criteria are answered directly. IPO and integration, in one short paragraph, because search consultants screen against a list.
  • It asks a question back. "Inherited or structural, and what does the chairman believe" is a senior question and establishes the conversation as between equals.
  • First-name salutation. Correct with a search partner you have dealt with; wrong with the client.
Sample TwoUnadvertised · Private group

No advertisement, no process. A direct letter to the person who can create the role, which at this level is how a large share of appointments actually happen.

Naila Qureshi
n.qureshi@executive.com  ·  +92 321 8890 114  ·  Lahore, Pakistan
12 August 2026
Mr. Tariq Sayeed Chairman Sayeed Group Lahore
Retail expansion — an introduction
Dear Mr. Sayeed,

I read that the group has taken a majority stake in two regional apparel chains and intends to consolidate them. I have done that work, and I am writing directly rather than waiting for a search process, because the useful conversation happens before the specification is written.

For the last six years I have been Managing Director of Zeen Retail, which I took from 22 stores to 86 and from PKR 1.9 billion to PKR 9.2 billion in revenue, returning 4.4 times to the founding investor. Gross margin moved less than a point across the whole expansion, which I mention because that is the number that usually gives way when a chain scales quickly.

Consolidating two acquired chains is a harder problem than opening stores. The systems will not match, the two teams will each believe their way is correct, and the cost of choosing slowly is higher than the cost of choosing wrongly. I would be glad to tell you how I would sequence the first hundred days.

If there is no role and no intention to create one, I would still value half an hour. I am in Lahore and can fit around your diary.

Kind regards,
Naila Qureshi
Enclosed: curriculum vitae (2 pages)
Why this one works
  • It opens with something he did in public. The acquisition. This is not flattery, it is evidence the letter was written for him specifically.
  • It names why it is unsolicited. "The useful conversation happens before the specification is written" — confident, true, and it disarms the obvious objection.
  • It offers a view on the hard part. Three sentences of genuine operating judgement about consolidation. This is what makes a chairman take a meeting.
  • It lowers the ask at the close. "If there is no role" removes the pressure to decide anything, which is exactly what makes half an hour easy to grant.
  • Margin held is volunteered. Pre-empting the scepticism about fast growth, before anyone raises it.
Sample ThreeIndependent director · Listed company

A board application is judged on independence, governance experience and what you add to the committee mix — not on operating achievements.

Saima Nawaz
s.nawaz@email.com  ·  +92 333 6612 118  ·  Karachi, Pakistan
12 August 2026
The Chair, Nomination Committee Gul Ahmed Textile Mills Limited Plot 82, Main National Highway Karachi
Expression of interest — Independent Director
Dear Chair,

I am writing to express interest in appointment as an independent director, following the notice of two forthcoming vacancies in the company's 2026 annual report.

I am a Fellow Chartered Accountant with twelve years as a financial controller, most recently for a PKR 12 billion revenue group. I rebuilt a control environment following two qualified audit opinions and delivered three consecutive clean opinions thereafter, and I hold the Certified Internal Auditor qualification. My contribution would be to the audit committee, where I would expect to chair in due course.

On independence: I hold no shareholding in the company, have no commercial relationship with it or its subsidiaries, and no family relationship with any director or sponsor. I have completed the SECP-mandated Directors' Training Programme.

I recognise that board composition is a question of balance rather than individual merit, and I would be glad to discuss whether the mix the committee is seeking is one I would improve.

Yours faithfully,
Saima Nawaz
Enclosed: curriculum vitae · Directors' Training Programme certificate
Why this one works
  • Independence is addressed explicitly and completely. Shareholding, commercial relationships, family relationships. The nomination committee must verify all three, and volunteering them saves a round of correspondence.
  • The contribution is named as a committee. "Audit committee, where I would expect to chair in due course" is specific about what she adds rather than general about her merit.
  • The mandatory training is stated. The SECP Directors' Training Programme is a regulatory requirement for listed boards in Pakistan, and having it already removes an obstacle.
  • The close acknowledges how boards are built. "Composition is a question of balance rather than individual merit" shows an understanding of governance that the rest of the letter only claims.

The register changes

A senior letter is not a more polished version of a junior one. The relationship is different, and so is almost everything about how it should read.

  • You are not asking for consideration. You are proposing a conversation between two people who each have something the other might want.
  • You may ask questions. A junior applicant answering questions is appropriate; a senior candidate who asks none reads as passive.
  • You may decline to be enthusiastic. Interest, yes. Eagerness, no — it inverts the relationship.
  • You address the business problem, not the job description. The specification is a symptom; the problem is what they are actually buying.
  • Length is earned by substance. Four paragraphs still. It is the density that rises, not the word count.

Writing to a search consultant

The consultant is an intermediary who will present you in a meeting you are not in, using words they can remember. Write for that.

  • Read the brief back to them in two or three sentences. It proves comprehension and it is unusual enough to be noticed.
  • Answer the essential criteria explicitly. They screen against a list, and an unanswered criterion is scored as absent.
  • Give them one repeatable sentence. The line they will use to describe you. Make it specific and slightly unexpected.
  • Be honest about gaps. They will find them, and a candidate who volunteers a gap is easier to present than one who is caught with it.
  • State availability and any constraint — notice period, location, compensation range if they have asked. Coyness wastes their time and yours.

The unsolicited approach

A large share of senior appointments are created rather than advertised, which means the letter that arrives before the process exists has an advantage no application can match.

It needs three things. A specific trigger — something the company did, announced or acquired, so the letter could not have been sent to anyone else. A view on the hard part of that problem, stated in three or four sentences with real judgement in it. And a small ask.

The small ask is what makes it work. "Half an hour, and if there is no role I would still value the conversation" is easy to grant and costs the recipient nothing to consider. "I would like to be considered for a position" asks them to decide something, and the easiest decision is no.

Propose a conversation, not an application.
