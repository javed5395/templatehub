Set 03 · Recommendation · Recommendation Letters

Character reference, kept honest.

A character reference is written by someone who has not employed you — a landlord, a community leader, a long-standing acquaintance — and it is asked for in situations where the stakes are unusually high. Vouching for someone means putting your own standing behind them, so the letter has to be careful about what it actually knows. Three samples: a tenancy, a visa application, and a court character reference.

Sample OneFormer landlord · Three years

The most common character reference and the easiest to write well, because everything in it is a matter of record.

Mr. Shahid Mehmood
House 22, Street 4, DHA Phase 5, Lahore  ·  s.mehmood@email.com  ·  +92 300 4471 220
12 August 2026
To Whom It May Concern
Tenancy Reference — Mr. Danish Raza
Dear Sir/Madam,

Danish Raza was my tenant at Flat 3-B, Block 7, Johar Town, Lahore, from 1 September 2022 until 31 August 2025 — a period of three years.

The monthly rent was PKR 42,000. It was paid in full on or before the fifth of every month for the entire tenancy, with no exceptions and no requests for extension. The security deposit was returned in full at the end of the tenancy, as there was no damage beyond normal wear.

I received no complaints from neighbours during the three years. He gave the full two months notice required under the agreement, and left the flat clean and in good order.

I would rent to him again without hesitation and am happy to confirm any of the above by telephone.

Yours faithfully,
Mr. Shahid Mehmood
Shahid Mehmood · Landlord · CNIC available on request
Why this one works
  • Every claim is a matter of record. Address, dates, rent amount, payment date, deposit outcome. Nothing requires an opinion.
  • "On or before the fifth of every month" is the whole letter. A specific payment pattern over three years is worth more than any adjective about reliability.
  • Absence of complaints is stated rather than implied. A next landlord is worried about neighbours, and this addresses it directly.
  • Notice and condition on leaving are included. These are what a landlord actually fears, and covering them makes the reference complete.
Sample TwoLong acquaintance · Sponsorship context

Supporting a visa application. The reader is a visa officer assessing credibility, so the letter must establish how the writer knows the applicant and be scrupulously factual.

Dr. Imran Qureshi
Consultant Gastroenterologist, Shaukat Khanum Memorial Hospital, Lahore  ·  i.qureshi@skm.org.pk  ·  +92 300 4471 990
12 August 2026
The Visa Officer Embassy of the Federal Republic of Germany Islamabad
Character reference in support of the visa application of Mr. Hamza Yousaf
Dear Sir/Madam,

I write in support of the student visa application of Hamza Yousaf. I have known him and his family for eleven years, initially as a patient of mine and subsequently through our shared membership of the Lahore Engineering Society, where I sit on the advisory committee.

I am a consultant physician at Shaukat Khanum Memorial Hospital and have practised in Lahore for fifteen years. My contact details and professional registration are given above and may be verified.

Hamza completed his mechanical engineering degree at UET Lahore in 2026 with first-class honours and is registered with the Pakistan Engineering Council. To my knowledge he has lived at the same family address in Model Town throughout the period I have known him, and his parents and two sisters remain resident in Lahore.

I can speak to his character with confidence. He has volunteered with a community welfare programme I help fund for four years, working most Sundays, and has never given me any reason to doubt his honesty or his intentions. He has spoken to me at length about his plan to complete a masters and return to work in the manufacturing sector here.

I am content to be contacted directly should the embassy wish to verify anything in this letter.

Yours faithfully,
Dr. Imran Qureshi
Dr. Imran Qureshi · PMDC Reg. 45-11204 · Shaukat Khanum Memorial Hospital, Lahore
Why this one works
  • The writer establishes his own credibility first. Profession, institution, registration number, years in practice. A visa officer weighs the referee before the reference.
  • The relationship is explained precisely. Eleven years, two contexts, both checkable. A vague "known him for many years" is worth nothing to an assessor.
  • Ties to Pakistan are addressed. Family address, resident parents and sisters, stated intention to return. This is what the officer is actually assessing.
  • "To my knowledge" is used. Qualifying second-hand facts protects the writer and reads as careful rather than evasive.
  • Verification is invited. A referee who offers to be contacted is a referee who believes what they wrote.
Sample ThreeSentencing · Highest stakes

The most serious version. A court reference must acknowledge the offence, avoid minimising it, and speak only to character. Getting this wrong actively harms the person it is meant to help.

Mrs. Nasreen Akhtar
Coordinator, Community Welfare Trust, Multan  ·  n.akhtar@cwtmultan.org  ·  +92 333 2218 006
12 August 2026
The Honourable Court District & Sessions Court, Multan
Character reference — Mr. Kashif Mehmood, case reference 4471/2026
May it please the Court,

I write as coordinator of the Community Welfare Trust, Multan, a position I have held for nine years. I have known Kashif Mehmood for six years as a volunteer with the Trust.

I understand that Mr. Mehmood has pleaded guilty to the offence before the Court. I have read nothing about the matter beyond what he has told me himself, and I do not seek to minimise it or to comment on it. I write only as to character, on the basis of what I have observed directly over six years.

Mr. Mehmood has volunteered at our distribution centre approximately two Saturdays a month since 2020, and has been responsible for the beneficiary register during that period. That register governs the distribution of goods to roughly four hundred families. He has handled it without any discrepancy arising in six years of quarterly checks.

He informed me of these proceedings himself, before I heard of them from any other source, and offered to step back from the register until the matter concluded. I declined that offer and he has continued in the role with the Trust's full knowledge.

I am aware that in writing this letter I am putting the Trust's name behind him, and I have considered that carefully. I remain willing to do so. I am content to attend Court or to be contacted should Your Honour require it.

Respectfully submitted,
Mrs. Nasreen Akhtar
Mrs. Nasreen Akhtar · Coordinator, Community Welfare Trust, Multan
Why this one works
  • It acknowledges the offence without discussing it. "I do not seek to minimise it or to comment on it." A court reference that argues the facts is dismissed and reflects badly on the defendant.
  • It states the limits of the writer's knowledge. "Nothing beyond what he has told me himself." Courts are alert to references written by people who do not know what happened.
  • The evidence is about trust with responsibility. Six years handling a register affecting four hundred families, no discrepancy. Directly relevant to character, and verifiable.
  • He disclosed it himself and offered to withdraw. This is the strongest fact in the letter, because it demonstrates the character trait rather than asserting it.
  • The writer names the cost to herself. Putting the Trust's name behind him, considered carefully, still willing. That sentence is why the letter carries weight.

Only what you actually know

A character reference asks you to lend your own standing to someone else, and the reader is assessing you as much as them. That means the letter must be scrupulous about the boundary between what you have observed and what you assume.

  • State how you know them, in what capacity, and for exactly how long.
  • Describe what you have personally witnessed, with specifics — the register, the rent, the volunteering, the dates.
  • Use "to my knowledge" for anything you have been told rather than seen.
  • Do not vouch for things outside your sight. A landlord cannot speak to someone's work ethic; a neighbour cannot speak to their finances.
  • Offer to be contacted, and mean it.

A short letter about what you genuinely know is worth far more than a long one that ranges beyond it, and a reader can tell the difference immediately.

Court references, specifically

A character reference for sentencing is a formal document read by a judge, and the most common mistakes actively harm the defendant.

  • Acknowledge the offence. A letter that appears unaware of what the person has done is given no weight at all.
  • Do not dispute the facts or minimise them. That is the advocate's job, not yours, and attempting it reads as poor judgement.
  • Do not comment on sentencing. Asking for leniency directly is generally unwelcome.
  • Speak only to character, from direct observation, with concrete examples.
  • State the limits of your knowledge of the matter itself.
  • Address it properly and include your full name, position and contact details.

And take direction from the defendant's lawyer before writing. Formats and expectations vary between courts, and a letter that does not follow the local convention may not be admitted.

When to say no

You are entitled to decline, and there are situations where declining is the right decision for both of you.

  • You do not know them well enough to say anything specific. A vague letter is worse than none and the reader will know why it is vague.
  • You have genuine reservations. A hedged reference is transparent, and it damages them more than silence.
  • The context is beyond what you can honestly speak to — being asked to vouch for financial reliability when you only know them socially.
  • Your own position makes it inappropriate, for professional or institutional reasons.

Decline early and plainly, so they can ask someone else while there is still time. "I do not think I know your work well enough to write something useful — you would be better served by someone who has managed you" is kind, honest and leaves the relationship intact.

Vouch only for what you have seen.
