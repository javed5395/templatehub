Sample One140 words · Sent as email body

Sent in the body of an email, resume attached. It has to work on a phone screen without scrolling, so the strongest fact is in the first line and nothing is repeated.

Ayesha Khan
ayesha.khan@email.com  ·  +92 300 4471 882  ·  Lahore, Pakistan
12 August 2026
Mr. Usman Javed Sales Manager Metro Distribution Lahore
Sales Coordinator — application (resume attached)
Dear Mr. Javed,

I rebuilt the quotation tracker at my current employer and cut quote turnaround from three days to one, for a six-person field team covering 140 retail accounts.

Your advertisement mentions the same bottleneck between the field team and the office. That is the part of the job I know well — two years of it, including maintaining a 900-record customer database at 99% accuracy.

My resume is attached. I can start within two weeks and I am happy to come in for a conversation whenever suits you.

Yours sincerely,
Ayesha Khan
Attached: resume (1 page)
Why this one works
  • 140 words, three paragraphs, no scrolling. On a phone the whole letter is visible at once, which is the only version that gets read in full.
  • The subject line does work. Role, action and "resume attached" in eight words. Inbox readers sort on the subject before opening anything.
  • Availability is stated. In a short letter this earns its line, because it removes a reply that would otherwise be needed.
  • Nothing is repeated from the resume except one number. Everything else is the connection to their advertisement.
Sample Two120 words · Pasted into a form

Pasted into a portal field with no formatting, often with a character limit. Written as plain sentences that survive stripping.

Danish Raza
danish.raza@email.com  ·  +92 340 8890 117  ·  Lahore, Pakistan
12 August 2026
Recruitment Team Ibex Global Lahore
Application — Team Leader, Customer Support
Dear Recruitment Team,

I handle 90 to 110 outbound calls a shift and met the monthly conversion target in ten of the last twelve months, with 98% attendance including night shifts.

I have trained two new agents on the dialler and the script, and I would like to do that formally as a team leader.

I am available for any shift pattern and can start immediately.

Yours sincerely,
Danish Raza
Why this one works
  • No formatting is required for it to work. No bullets, no bold, no indentation. Portals strip all of that and letters written around it arrive broken.
  • The attendance figure survives the cut. At 120 words every line must earn its place, and for shift work attendance outranks almost everything.
  • The ambition is stated plainly. "I would like to do that formally as a team leader" — one sentence connecting what he already does to the post applied for.
  • Addressed generically but correctly. When there is genuinely no name, "Dear Recruitment Team" is better than "To Whom It May Concern", which reads as a form letter.
Sample Three110 words · After an unanswered application

Two weeks of silence. A short follow-up that adds something new rather than asking for a status update, which is the version that occasionally works.

Laiba Tariq
laiba.tariq@email.com  ·  +92 321 4405 992  ·  Karachi, Pakistan
12 August 2026
Ms. Hina Sajid Marketing Lead Cheetay Lahore
Following up — Marketing Intern application, 28 July
Dear Ms. Sajid,

I applied for the marketing internship two weeks ago and wanted to add one thing rather than ask for an update.

Since applying, the society account I run passed 7,400 followers, and the bakery client I manage went from 12 to 40 enquiries a month. Both are small, and both are mine end to end.

If the role is filled, I would still be glad to be considered for anything later in the year.

Yours sincerely,
Laiba Tariq
Why this one works
  • It adds information instead of requesting a reply. "I wanted to add one thing rather than ask for an update" — the difference between a nudge and a reason to reopen the file.
  • The update is genuinely new. Numbers that changed since the application, which proves the work is ongoing rather than a snapshot.
  • It is honest about scale. "Both are small, and both are mine end to end." Owning the modesty makes the claim land.
  • The close removes pressure. Offering to be considered later makes it easy to answer, and easy answers get sent.

When short is right

A full-page letter is right when a named person will read it before deciding — a senior post, a small employer, a direct approach. Everywhere else it is the wrong instrument.

  • Portal applications where the letter is a text field. Assume nobody scrolls it.
  • Email applications read on a phone.
  • High-volume roles where a recruiter is processing dozens an hour.
  • Recruitment agencies, which frequently forward only the resume anyway.
  • Follow-ups and speculative notes, where length reads as pressure.

Short is not lesser. A 140-word letter that is read completely beats a 400-word letter that is abandoned after two lines, and the second outcome is far more common than applicants think.

What survives the cut

Cutting a letter to 150 words is an exercise in deciding what actually mattered. Keep these, in this order:

  • One number or fact, your strongest, in the first sentence.
  • One line connecting it to their advertisement — the problem they described.
  • One practical fact — availability, notice period, location, or the referral name.
  • One easy close.

Delete without hesitation: any sentence about your enthusiasm, any adjective about yourself, any restatement of your job history, any thanks for their time and consideration, and the phrase "I believe I would be a great fit".

A useful test: if a sentence could appear in an application to a completely different company, it is not doing work. Cut it.

The email itself

When the letter is an email, the envelope matters as much as the contents.

  • Subject line: role, then application, then reference number if there is one. "Sales Coordinator — application (resume attached)". Never leave it blank or write "Job application".
  • Attach the resume as PDF unless the advert asks for Word, and name the file properly: Ayesha-Khan-Resume.pdf, not resume-final-2.pdf.
  • Put the letter in the body, not as a second attachment. One click is one click too many.
  • Send from a professional address. Firstname.lastname is fine; a nickname from 2011 is not.
  • Send on a weekday morning if you have the choice. It is a small effect and it is free.
