Set 01 · Reference Sheets · Reference Pages

The clean reference page, set to match.

The reference page is the document that arrives after the interview went well, and it is almost always improvised in five minutes. It should use the same header as your resume so the pair reads as one document, and it should tell the reader what each referee can actually speak to. Three samples: professional, academic, and mixed for a career changer.

Sample OneMid-career · Three referees

Three referees covering three different angles — the current manager, a peer from another function, and someone who was managed. That spread is deliberate.

k.nadeem@nishatmills.com · +92 300 4471 118 · Can speak to month-end close, the reconciliation audit and team supervision. Please contact only after an offer is discussed.
Ms. Sadia Rehman — Internal Audit Manager, Nishat Textile Mills
Cross-functional colleague, 2022 —
s.rehman@nishatmills.com · +92 321 7719 004 · Worked with me on three annual audits; can speak to controls, documentation and audit readiness.
Mr. Adnan Iqbal — Accounts Officer, Packages Limited
Reported to me, 2020 — 2022
adnan.iqbal@email.com · +92 333 2218 447 · Can speak to how I supervise, train and review work.
Mr. Faisal Butt — Former Finance Manager, Packages Limited
Direct manager, 2019 — 2022
f.butt@email.com · +92 345 8890 117 · Can speak to the receivables ageing work and my early career development.

Notes for the Reader

All four referees are aware of this application and expect contact.
Written references from Mr. Butt and Ms. Rehman are available on request.
My current employer is not aware I am looking; please give notice before contacting Mr. Nadeem.
Why this one works
  • The header matches the resume exactly. Same name treatment, same contact line. The two documents read as one file rather than two attachments.
  • Each referee has a stated relationship and period. "Direct manager, 2022 —" tells the reader how much weight to give the reference before they call.
  • What each can speak to is named. This is the most useful and most commonly missing element. It lets a recruiter call the right person about the right thing.
  • One referee reported to him. Upward references are rare and persuasive — how someone manages is hard to establish any other way.
  • The confidentiality instruction is explicit and repeated. A current employer contacted without warning is the single worst outcome of a reference page.

Academic references work differently — the referee's standing matters, and the relationship needs to be described in academic terms.

Dr. Hina Rehman — Associate Professor, Institute of Environmental Engineering
Principal Investigator, HEC project, 2024 —
h.rehman@uet.edu.pk · +92 300 8814 227 · I work on her HEC-funded membrane bioreactor project. Can speak to laboratory practice, data handling and reliability.
Prof. Dr. Nigel Wright — Professor of Water Engineering, University of Leeds
External examiner, MS thesis, 2024
n.wright@leeds.ac.uk · Examined my thesis and has since corresponded with me on the fractionation work. Can offer an independent external view.

Notes for the Reader

Prof. Saleem has agreed to submit directly through any online referee portal.
Letters can be provided in advance if the application requires them at submission.
All three are aware of the specific programmes I am applying to.
Why this one works
  • The supervisor is first and identified as such. Admissions committees weigh the supervisor's letter above all others and look for it immediately.
  • An external referee is included. The examiner from another institution provides independence, which internal referees structurally cannot.
  • Institutional email addresses are used. An academic referee writing from a personal address is quietly discounted; the university domain is part of the credential.
  • Portal submission is addressed. Most academic applications collect references through a system, and confirming a referee will use it removes a common failure point.

Notes for the Reader

The two corporate referees are the most relevant to this application; the teaching referees cover the longer record.
All four are aware of this application and expect contact.
Why this one works
  • The referees are grouped and labelled by field. The reader is told immediately which are relevant and which provide the longer history.
  • The internal client is included. Someone whose team went through her programme can answer "did it work", which a manager cannot answer as convincingly.
  • A mentee appears again. For a training role, evidence from someone she developed is directly on point.
  • The note explains the split rather than apologising for it. "Most relevant" and "longer record" frames it as deliberate, which it is.

Who to choose

Most people list whoever managed them most recently and stop. A better page covers different angles, because a recruiter is trying to triangulate rather than confirm.

  • A direct manager. Unavoidable and expected. Ideally recent.
  • A peer from another function. Someone who worked with you but did not depend on you. Their view is read as less biased.
  • Someone who reported to you, if you have managed anyone. How you manage is the hardest thing to establish and this is the only clean way to do it.
  • A client or internal customer, if your work has one. They answer "did it actually work".
  • An academic supervisor, for research or graduate applications.

Three names is standard, four is fine, five looks anxious. And never list someone you have not asked — the call will happen, and being unprepared for it is worse than having no reference.

What goes beside each name

Five things, and the last one is the one nobody includes.

  • Full name and current title, with the organisation.
  • The relationship, stated plainly: direct manager, colleague, reported to me, client, supervisor.
  • The period of that relationship.
  • Email and phone. A work email where possible.
  • What they can speak to. One sentence naming the specific things this person witnessed.

That last line is what turns a list of names into a useful document. It lets a recruiter with time for one call choose the right person, and it quietly reminds them what your strengths were supposed to be.

Protecting your current job

The most damaging thing a reference page can do is get your current employer called before you are ready. It happens, it happens quickly, and it cannot be undone.

Two protections, and use both. Put the instruction in the profile line at the top where it cannot be missed, and repeat it against the specific referee. "Please contact only after an offer is discussed" in bold, next to your current manager's name.

If you would rather not list a current employer at all, say so explicitly rather than leaving a gap: "A reference from my current employer can be provided once an offer is agreed." A stated boundary is read as professional. An unexplained absence is read as a problem.
