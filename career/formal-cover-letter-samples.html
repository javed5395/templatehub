Classic professional, correctly formal.

Government departments, banks, law firms and public sector organisations read letters against a convention, and a letter that ignores it is read as a candidate who does not understand the environment. Formality here is not decoration — it is evidence of fit. Three samples: a government post, a banking application, and a law firm.

Sample OneAdvertised through a public notice

Public sector applications are assessed against advertised criteria, often by a committee scoring each one. The letter should make the scoring easy.

Muhammad Adnan Sheikh
adnan.sheikh@email.com  ·  +92 300 8814 220  ·  Islamabad, Pakistan  ·  CNIC 61101-4471882-3
12 August 2026
The Deputy Director (Administration) Ministry of Planning, Development & Special Initiatives Block P, Pak Secretariat Islamabad
Application for the post of Assistant Director (Monitoring & Evaluation), BPS-17 — Advertisement No. 14/2026 dated 28 July 2026
Respected Sir/Madam,

With reference to the above-mentioned advertisement published in the daily Dawn on 28 July 2026, I wish to apply for the post of Assistant Director (Monitoring & Evaluation), BPS-17.

I hold an MSc in Economics from Quaid-i-Azam University (2019, First Division) and have six years of relevant experience, presently serving as Monitoring Officer at the Punjab Planning and Development Board. In this capacity I have conducted field monitoring of 34 development schemes with a cumulative allocation of PKR 4.2 billion, and prepared quarterly progress reports submitted to the provincial steering committee.

The advertised requirements of monitoring experience, report preparation and familiarity with PC-I and PC-IV documentation are all met by my present duties, and full particulars are set out in the enclosed curriculum vitae along with attested copies of testimonials.

I shall be grateful for the opportunity to appear before the selection committee and to furnish any further information required.

Yours faithfully,
Muhammad Adnan Sheikh
Enclosed: (1) Curriculum vitae (2) Attested copies of educational documents (3) Experience certificates (4) CNIC copy (5) Two passport-size photographs
Why this one works
  • The subject line quotes the advertisement exactly. Post, scale, advertisement number and date. Public sector applications are matched against a register, and a mismatch causes rejection before assessment.
  • Requirements are answered in the advert's own words. "Monitoring experience, report preparation, PC-I and PC-IV" — a scoring committee can tick each one without interpreting.
  • Scale is quantified even in a formal register. 34 schemes, PKR 4.2 billion. Formality does not require vagueness, and most public sector letters are vague.
  • The enclosure list is numbered. Incomplete documentation is the single largest cause of rejection in government recruitment, and listing them proves the file is complete.
  • "Yours faithfully" with "Sir/Madam". The convention pairs — faithfully with an unnamed recipient, sincerely with a named one. Getting it wrong is noticed in these environments.
Sample TwoNamed recipient · Commercial bank

Banking sits between formal and commercial. The register stays conservative but the content is measured, because the reader is running a P&L.

Nadia Aslam
nadia.aslam@email.com  ·  +92 321 4405 118  ·  Karachi, Pakistan
12 August 2026
Mr. Kashif Mahmood Head of Branch Operations Bank Alfalah Limited B.A. Building, I.I. Chundrigar Road Karachi
Application — Manager, Branch Operations
Dear Mr. Mahmood,

I am writing to apply for the position of Manager, Branch Operations, advertised on your careers portal on 30 July 2026.

I have nine years in retail banking operations, most recently as Branch Operations Officer at a branch handling 1,100 daily transactions and PKR 40 million in cash flow. During this period I reduced account opening turnaround from 48 hours to 6 by restructuring the KYC checklist, and closed three consecutive internal audits with no exceptions raised.

I hold an MBA in Banking and Finance and am AML/CFT certified through NIBAF. I am familiar with Temenos T24 and have supervised teams of up to four tellers, including end-of-day reconciliation and cash management.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how this experience would apply to your branch network, and I have enclosed my curriculum vitae for your consideration.

Yours sincerely,
Nadia Aslam
Enclosed: curriculum vitae · certificates available on request
Why this one works
  • Audit outcome is the strongest line. "Three consecutive internal audits with no exceptions" is the sentence a banking operations head reads for, and very few candidates write it.
  • The core system is named. Temenos T24 separates candidates who need training from those who do not, and in banking that difference is months.
  • Compliance certification is explicit. AML/CFT through NIBAF is a regulatory requirement, not a nice-to-have, and it belongs in the body rather than the CV alone.
  • The opening is conventional and that is correct here. Banking is not the environment for an unconventional first line — but the second paragraph carries real numbers, which is where the letter separates itself.
Sample ThreeAssociate application · Chambers

Legal applications are judged partly on the writing itself. Precision, structure and restraint in the letter are read as evidence of how you will draft.

Hassan Mahmood
hassan.mahmood@email.com  ·  +92 333 2218 447  ·  Lahore, Pakistan  ·  Enrolled, Lahore High Court (2023)
12 August 2026
Mr. Faisal Siddiqui Senior Partner Siddiqui & Associates 14-A Main Boulevard, Gulberg III Lahore
Application — Associate, Corporate and Commercial
Dear Mr. Siddiqui,

I am an advocate enrolled with the Lahore High Court in 2023, currently in my third year of practice, and I write to apply for an associate position in your corporate and commercial team.

My present practice is predominantly transactional. Over the last two years I have drafted and negotiated 40 commercial agreements, including distribution, licensing and services contracts, and assisted on four share purchase transactions ranging from PKR 60 million to PKR 400 million. I have also conducted due diligence on eleven target companies and prepared the corresponding reports.

I appeared before the Lahore High Court in nine matters as junior counsel, largely in company law and recovery proceedings, and I maintain a working familiarity with SECP filing requirements from my present firm's compliance practice.

Your firm's corporate practice, and particularly its work on cross-border joint ventures, is the area I wish to develop in. I have enclosed my curriculum vitae and would be glad to provide drafting samples on request.

Yours sincerely,
Hassan Mahmood
Enclosed: curriculum vitae · drafting samples available on request
Why this one works
  • Enrolment year is in the letterhead. Call to the bar and year of enrolment establish seniority in legal practice more precisely than years of experience.
  • Transactional work is counted and sized. Forty agreements, four share purchases, eleven due diligence exercises, with transaction values. Legal experience is otherwise impossible to compare between candidates.
  • Court appearances are separated from transactional work. Nine matters as junior counsel tells a partner exactly what kind of lawyer this is.
  • Drafting samples are offered. In legal hiring the writing is the product, and offering samples signals confidence in it.
  • The letter is itself restrained and precisely structured. For a legal application the prose is part of the evidence.

The conventions that are checked

In formal environments a handful of small conventions are noticed, and getting them wrong signals unfamiliarity more loudly than anything you say about yourself.

  • "Yours faithfully" pairs with an unnamed recipient (Dear Sir/Madam). "Yours sincerely" pairs with a named one (Dear Mr. Siddiqui). Never mix them.
  • Quote the advertisement precisely — post title, scale or grade, reference number, publication and date.
  • Full recipient address block, including department and building where relevant.
  • Numbered enclosure list, especially in public sector applications.
  • No contractions. "I do not" rather than "I don't".
  • No first-name informality unless invited.

None of this makes a weak application strong. All of it prevents a strong one being set aside for a reason nobody will ever tell you.

The documentation problem

In Pakistani public sector recruitment, incomplete documentation rejects more applications than weak credentials do — and the rejection is administrative, so no feedback follows.

Read the advertisement and list every document it requires, then list them again as a numbered enclosure at the foot of your letter. Typically: attested educational certificates, experience certificates, CNIC copy, domicile, photographs, and the application fee receipt where applicable.

"Attested" means attested. A photocopy where an attested copy was required is treated as a missing document, and the file is set aside before anyone reads a word about your experience.

Formal is not vague

The most common failure in formal letters is assuming that dignified language means empty language. It does not, and committees notice the difference.

These two sentences occupy the same register:

Weak

I have considerable experience in the monitoring and evaluation of development projects and have discharged my duties with diligence and dedication.

Strong

I have conducted field monitoring of 34 development schemes with a cumulative allocation of PKR 4.2 billion, and prepared quarterly progress reports submitted to the provincial steering committee.

The second is equally formal and carries five times the information. Formality governs the grammar and the courtesy. It does not require you to say nothing.

Follow the form, and still say something.
