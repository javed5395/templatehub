Professional resignation, with handover.

At senior level the resignation letter is only half the document. What decides whether you leave with a reference and a relationship is the handover plan attached to it — and the person who writes their own handover plan controls how their departure is remembered. Three samples: a manager, a technical lead, and a department head.

Sample OneTwo months notice · 68 staff

A senior operations role with a long notice period. The letter is short; the value is in the plan it proposes.

Imran Sheikh
imran.sheikh@email.com  ·  +92 333 6612 907
12 August 2026
Ms. Saima Rehman General Manager, Operations Northline Logistics Lahore
Notice of Resignation — Operations Manager
Dear Saima,

I am writing to give formal notice of my resignation as Operations Manager. My contractual notice is two months, placing my final working day on Friday, 9 October 2026.

Five years here, and the last two running the three centres, have been the best stretch of my working life. I am leaving for a regional role with wider scope, not because anything here has gone wrong.

I would like to use the notice period properly rather than let it drift. My proposal, subject to whatever you prefer: weeks one to three, document every process I own and complete the quarter-end reporting. Weeks four to six, run the recruitment and shadowing for my successor if you appoint internally. Weeks seven and eight, step back into a support role so the new person is actually running it while I am still here to be asked.

I will have the full handover pack ready by the end of month one so you can see it early rather than at the end. Whatever you decide, I would rather leave this in good order than leave quietly.

With genuine thanks,
Imran Sheikh
To follow: handover pack — processes, contacts, calendar, open issues
Why this one works
  • The notice period is structured into phases. Document, train, step back. This is what separates a professional exit from two months of gradual disengagement.
  • The last phase is the clever one. Handing over control while still present means problems surface before you leave rather than after.
  • It says the pack will be ready in month one. Early visibility lets the manager check it is adequate while there is still time to fix gaps.
  • "Subject to whatever you prefer." Proposing a plan without imposing one. She may have her own view, and the offer is what matters.
  • The reason is given in one clause and is not a complaint. "Wider scope, not because anything has gone wrong" pre-empts the assumption every manager makes.
Sample TwoOne month · Knowledge concentrated in one head

The dangerous kind of departure — the person who knows how things work. The letter addresses the risk explicitly, which is what a technical manager most needs to hear.

Hamza Ali
hamza.ali@email.com  ·  +92 333 9014 662
12 August 2026
Mr. Yasir Ahmed Engineering Manager Systems Limited Karachi
Notice of Resignation — Software Engineer
Dear Yasir,

This is my formal notice of resignation. My notice period is one month and my final working day will be Friday, 11 September 2026.

I know the payments integration and the API layer are largely in my head, and I would rather fix that before I go than leave you to discover it in October. I have started writing it down already.

By the end of week two I will have three documents ready: the payments integration runbook including the failure modes we hit last year, an architecture note on the API layer covering the read-replica work and why it is shaped the way it is, and the on-call playbook for the services I own. I will then walk two engineers through each one, so it is in two other heads and not just on paper.

I am happy to remain contactable for urgent questions for a month after I leave, and I mean that as a genuine offer rather than a formality. Four years here have been good ones.

With thanks,
Hamza Ali
To follow: payments runbook · API architecture note · on-call playbook
Why this one works
  • It names the risk before the manager has to. "Largely in my head" — the exact thing his manager is worried about, said first and without defensiveness.
  • Documentation is paired with walkthroughs. "In two other heads and not just on paper" shows an understanding that documents alone do not transfer knowledge.
  • The failure modes are included. Runbooks that only cover the happy path are the useless kind, and saying so signals real experience.
  • The post-departure offer is qualified as genuine. Everyone says it and almost nobody means it; naming that makes it credible.
Sample ThreeThree months · School department

Leaving a school mid-year is disruptive by nature, so the letter is built around protecting the students rather than the department.

Nadia Hussain
n.hussain@email.com  ·  +92 300 4471 883
12 August 2026
Mr. Tariq Mahmood Vice Principal, Academics Roots International School Islamabad
Notice of Resignation — Head of Science
Dear Mr. Mahmood,

I am writing to resign from my position as Head of Science, with three months notice as my contract requires. My final working day would fall on 12 November 2026.

I am conscious that this falls mid-term. If it is workable for the school, I would prefer to stay to the end of the winter term on 19 December, so that the O-Level sections are not handed over between mocks and the final examination. I have discussed this with my next employer and they have agreed to it if we need it.

Whatever we decide, I will leave the department in order: the schemes of work for all three subjects are current, the practical programme is documented with the technician schedule, and I will write handover notes on each of the nine teachers including where each is in their appraisal cycle.

Five years here have mattered a great deal to me, and the science department is in far better shape than I found it, which is largely down to the freedom you gave me to rebuild it.

With respect and thanks,
Nadia Hussain
To follow: schemes of work · practical programme documentation · staff handover notes
Why this one works
  • She offers to stay longer than required. Six weeks beyond her notice, to protect the examination cohort. This is the strongest possible exit and costs her nothing but time.
  • The new employer has already agreed. Saying so makes the offer real rather than theoretical, which is what allows the school to accept it.
  • Staff handover notes include appraisal position. A successor inheriting nine teachers needs to know where each stands, and nobody thinks to leave that.
  • The credit is shared at the close. "Largely down to the freedom you gave me" — gracious, true, and it is the sentence he will remember when asked for a reference.

Why write your own handover

Most people treat the notice period as a countdown. The organisation treats it as the last evidence of what kind of colleague you were, and that evidence is what a reference call three years later is actually based on.

There is a practical argument too. If you do not define the handover, someone else will define it for you — usually as an open-ended series of interruptions, meetings you did not schedule and questions after you have gone. A written plan converts that into a finite, controllable piece of work.

And it protects your reputation in the one place it is most fragile. Departments remember the people who left things broken far longer than they remember the people who did the job well.

What a handover pack contains

  • Processes you own, written as steps someone else can follow, including the parts that are only in your head.
  • Failure modes. What breaks, how often, what you do about it. The most valuable and most commonly omitted section.
  • The calendar. Monthly, quarterly and annual deadlines. Closes, filings, audits, renewals, reporting cycles.
  • Contacts. Who to call at every supplier, client, department and authority — with names, not just organisations.
  • Open issues. Everything unresolved, honestly, including the ones that reflect badly.
  • Access and credentials, routed properly through IT rather than left in a document.
  • People notes, if you manage anyone. Where each person is, what they are working towards.

Write it for someone competent who knows nothing about your job specifically. That is exactly who will be reading it.

Phasing the notice period

A structured notice period has three phases, whatever its length.

  • Document. First third. Write everything down while you still have full context and before farewell lunches start consuming days.
  • Transfer. Middle third. Walk people through it. Documents alone do not move knowledge — a conversation over the document does.
  • Step back. Final third. Let the successor run it while you are still available to be asked. This is the phase everyone skips and it is the one that determines whether the handover actually worked.

Propose the phases in your letter, subject to your manager's preference. Even if they change it entirely, you have set the tone of the departure in the first paragraph they read.

Leave it in good order.
