Set 01 · Modern · Cover Letters

The modern cover letter, opened properly.

Most cover letters are read for four seconds and thrown away, because they open by announcing that you are applying for a job the reader already knows you applied for. A letter earns its place only by saying something the resume cannot. Three samples, each with a different opening that works.

Sample OneMarketing manager · Advertised post

The most reliable opening: lead with the single strongest fact about you, before any pleasantry. The reader is deciding whether to keep reading during that sentence.

Sana Iqbal
sana.iqbal@email.com  ·  +92 302 7741 663  ·  Islamabad, Pakistan
12 August 2026
Ms. Farah Naz Head of Marketing Shan Foods Karachi
Application — Marketing Manager (Ref: MKT-2026-14)
Dear Ms. Naz,

In my current role I cut blended cost per lead from PKR 640 to PKR 310 while growing organic traffic 3.4 times. Your advertisement asks for someone who can bring acquisition cost down without cutting volume, and that is the problem I have spent four years on.

At Bykea I own a PKR 4M annual paid budget across search and social. The saving came from two decisions rather than better bidding: moving 40% of spend into content that already ranked, and killing three campaigns that looked profitable on last-click and were not on any other model. The second decision was unpopular for a quarter and then obviously right.

Before that I managed PKR 1.8M quarterly at Zameen.com at a 3.1 return on ad spend, and built the abandoned-enquiry email flow that recovered 12% of lost leads. I mention it because your listing mentions lifecycle marketing, and that flow is the closest thing I have to it.

I know Shan is moving more spend into measurable channels this year. I would like to talk about where that money should go and, more usefully, where it should not.

Yours sincerely,
Sana Iqbal
Enclosed: resume (2 pages)
Why this one works
  • The first sentence is a number. No "I am writing to apply". The reader learns something in four seconds, which is all the time the letter had.
  • It names the problem from the advert. "Bring acquisition cost down without cutting volume" proves the advert was read rather than skimmed.
  • The middle paragraph explains how. Two decisions, one of them unpopular. This is what a resume bullet cannot carry and why the letter exists.
  • It closes with a view, not a request. "Where that money should not go" is an opinion, and opinions invite conversation in a way "I look forward to hearing from you" never does.
  • Addressed to a named person. Two minutes on LinkedIn. It changes how the letter reads more than any sentence in it.
Sample TwoSoftware engineer · Company you admire

When your numbers are good but not unusual, lead with a specific reason for wanting this employer — specific enough that it could not be sent to anyone else.

Hamza Ali
hamza.ali@email.com  ·  +92 333 9014 662  ·  Karachi, Pakistan
12 August 2026
Mr. Yasir Ahmed Engineering Manager Careem Karachi
Application — Senior Software Engineer, Platform
Dear Mr. Ahmed,

I read your engineering post on cutting p95 latency by moving read traffic off the primary, and recognised the problem — I did a smaller version of it last year and got median response from 800ms to 320ms. Yours was the harder case and the writeup was the reason I applied.

For the last three years I have worked on the API layer at Systems Limited: a Node and Postgres service handling around 15,000 payment transactions a month. The latency work was the interesting part, but the change I am proudest of is less glamorous — I took automated test coverage from 34% to 81%, which halved the hotfixes we shipped on Fridays.

I have not worked at your scale. I have read enough of your engineering writing to know roughly what I would be walking into, and I would rather join a team that publishes its mistakes than one that does not.

My GitHub is on my resume, including the small library I maintain. I would be glad to talk about the read-replica work, or about anything in the codebase you would want a new engineer to fix first.

Yours sincerely,
Hamza Ali
Enclosed: resume (1 page) · github.com/hamzaali
Why this one works
  • It refers to something specific the company published. Not "I admire your innovative culture" — an actual engineering post, and a technical connection to his own work.
  • It admits a gap. "I have not worked at your scale." This is disarming, honest, and it makes everything else in the letter more believable.
  • The proudest achievement is the unglamorous one. Test coverage over latency. Engineering managers notice candidates who value the boring reliability work.
  • The close offers a topic. "Anything you would want a new engineer to fix first" is an invitation to a real conversation.
Sample ThreeOperations manager · Referred by someone

If a person told you about this job, that belongs in the first line. A referral is the strongest opening available and most people bury it in paragraph three.

Imran Sheikh
imran.sheikh@email.com  ·  +92 333 6612 907  ·  Lahore, Pakistan
12 August 2026
Ms. Saima Rehman General Manager, Operations Northline Logistics Lahore
Application — Regional Operations Manager
Dear Ms. Rehman,

Adeel Bhatti suggested I write to you. He and I worked opposite sides of the same route network for two years at National Foods, and he mentioned you are looking for someone to take the northern distribution centres.

I currently run three centres, 68 staff and a PKR 240M operating budget. Over two years we took on-time dispatch from 82% to 96% and removed PKR 31M of annual cost, and I want to be clear that we did the second without a single redundancy — we consolidated routes and renegotiated two contracts instead.

The part of your advertisement that interested me is the line about integrating the two newly acquired depots. I have not done an integration at that scale, but I rebuilt a shift roster across three sites last year that cut overtime spend 34% while holding output flat, and the hard part of that was the same: persuading people who had done it one way for years.

I am happy to speak whenever suits, and Adeel is glad to be contacted if that is useful.

Yours sincerely,
Imran Sheikh
Enclosed: resume (1 page)
Why this one works
  • The referral is the first four words. It is the strongest asset in the letter and it belongs where it cannot be missed.
  • It pre-empts a suspicion. "Without a single redundancy" answers the question every operations reader has about a cost-cutting claim before they can form it.
  • It names what he has not done. Then supplies the nearest equivalent and identifies the genuinely hard part. This reads as judgement rather than salesmanship.
  • The referee is offered. "Adeel is glad to be contacted" makes the referral verifiable, which is what turns it from a name-drop into evidence.

What the letter is for

A cover letter has exactly one job: say something true and useful that the resume cannot say. If your letter summarises your resume in prose, it is not a letter — it is a slower copy of a document the reader already has.

Three things only a letter can do.

  • Explain how, not just what. A resume says cost fell 31%. A letter says which two decisions caused it and which one was unpopular.
  • Address the specific employer. Why this company, this team, this problem — with a detail that could not be copied into another application.
  • Handle the awkward thing. A gap, a change of field, a location, a missing qualification. One sentence, unapologetic, and then move on.

If none of those three apply to your situation, a short letter is better than a long one, and no letter is better than a padded one.

Four openings that work

The first sentence decides whether the rest is read. These four all work, and all of them begin with information rather than announcement.

  • The number. "I cut cost per lead from PKR 640 to PKR 310." Best when your strongest fact is genuinely strong.
  • The referral. "Adeel Bhatti suggested I write to you." Best when true. Put it first.
  • The specific reason. A thing the company published, built or announced, and why it connects to your work.
  • The problem. "Your advertisement describes a team that ships fortnightly and wants to ship daily. I have made exactly that transition twice."

And the one to delete permanently: "I am writing to apply for the position of X as advertised on Y." The reader knows. It is the envelope, not the letter.

The four-paragraph shape

Every sample on this page uses the same structure, and it holds for almost any application.

  • One: the strongest fact, referral or reason. Two or three sentences maximum.
  • Two: your evidence — what you own now, with the numbers, and how the result happened.
  • Three: the connection to them — the line in the advert you are answering, and honestly what you have not done.
  • Four: a close that offers something. A view, a topic, an available referee.

One page. Never two. If it runs long, the middle paragraph is the one carrying too much — the detail belongs in the resume, and the letter should keep only what explains it.

Three rewrites

Weak

I am writing to express my keen interest in the Marketing Manager position advertised on your website.

Strong

In my current role I cut blended cost per lead from PKR 640 to PKR 310 while growing organic traffic 3.4 times.

The first sentence uses the most valuable line on the page to state something already known.

Weak

I am a hard-working and dedicated professional with excellent communication skills and a passion for excellence.

Strong

The saving came from two decisions rather than better bidding: moving 40% of spend into content that already ranked, and killing three campaigns that looked profitable on last-click.

Adjectives about yourself carry no information. The mechanism does, and it is the only thing the letter can add.

Weak

I look forward to hearing from you at your earliest convenience and thank you for your time and consideration.

Strong

I would like to talk about where that money should go and, more usefully, where it should not.

A polite non-statement, or a reason to reply. Only one of them gets a call.
