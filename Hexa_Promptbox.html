AI Capabilities

One engine. Six creative surfaces.

Hexa is not a single tool — it is a unified intelligence that operates across every format your team ships. From investor narratives to brand systems, the same engine understands your intent and outputs production-ready work.

01

Generate Pitch Decks

Describe your startup, market, and round. Hexa drafts a 10–14 slide narrative deck with traction, TAM, and ask — ready to send to investors.

02

Create Media Kits

Press-ready media kits with brand story, leadership bios, logo lockups, fact sheet, and high-res image gallery — formatted for journalists and partners.

03

Convert Canva ↔ PPTX

Lossless two-way conversion between Canva and PowerPoint. Fonts, gradients, animations, and master layouts survive the round trip — no rebuild required.

04

Smart Template Search

Semantic search across 180k+ templates. Hexa understands intent, industry, and aesthetic — surfacing the three best matches before you finish typing.

05

Auto Fill Content

Drop in a URL, brief, or spreadsheet. Hexa extracts key facts, writes slide copy, generates charts, and fills every placeholder with on-brand content.

06

Brand Consistency Engine

Upload a brand kit once. Every generated asset — across every format — auto-enforces type scale, color tokens, voice, and logo placement rules.
