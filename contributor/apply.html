Step 4 — Getting paid

We ask now because payment options differ by country. Knowing this up front means we never approve someone we then cannot pay.

We pay through Whop. It is free, it arrives quickly, and none of it is lost to bank charges or currency conversion — so more of your money reaches you. If you don't have a Whop account yet you can open one free when you're approved.

Don't send any account numbers or wallet addresses here. We ask for those only when your first payment is due.

Step 5 — Identity check

Because we send you money, we need to confirm you are a real person and that the name matches the account we pay. Upload a clear photo of your government ID — CNIC, passport or driving licence.

Images only, up to 8 MB each. Stored privately — only our review team can open them, they are never shown on the site, and we delete them automatically as soon as your application is decided — whether we approve it or not.

Step 6 — The deal

You earn 80% of the sale price on every sale of your own template — 75% on templates priced at $5 or less.

We pay the payment processing fees, not you. Card charges and currency costs come out of our commission, never your share.

If you choose to offer marketers a commission for promoting your template, that comes out of your side — you set the rate, so it is your money spent on promotion. Sales with no marketer simply pay you the full 80%.

Earnings are held for 20 days after the buyer pays, then become available. We send payment once your available balance reaches $50.

You keep ownership of your designs. You confirm the work is yours and that you have the right to sell it — including every font, photo and icon inside it. We review every submission and may decline anything that does not meet our standards.

Read the full contributor terms — worked examples of what you earn, refunds, and how either of us can end the arrangement.
