Contributor Terms

Plain English, and written to match exactly what our system actually does. Last updated 9 August 2026.

The short version. You design templates. We review them, sell them, and handle payments, hosting and support. You keep the copyright. You earn 80% of the sale price (75% on templates at $5 or less), and we pay the payment processing fees, not you. Money is released 20 days after a buyer pays, and we send it once your balance reaches $50.

1. Applying

The programme is application-only. We look at your portfolio and decide. We may decline an application without giving a detailed reason, and we may close the programme to new applicants at any time.

We ask for identity documents to confirm you are a real person we can pay. We delete those images once your application is decided. We do not need them after that, so we do not keep them.

2. What you may submit

By submitting a template you confirm, and we rely on you confirming, that:

  • You created it, and you own the rights to it.
  • Every font, photo, icon and graphic in it is either yours, or licensed for commercial resale as part of a template. This is where most problems start — a font you may use in your own work is often not licensed for you to sell inside a product.
  • It does not use anyone's trademark, brand or copyrighted material without permission.
  • It is not substantially a copy of someone else's template.
If a rights complaint is made about your template, we will take it off sale while we look into it. If the complaint is valid, any unpaid earnings from that template are withheld, and you are responsible for any costs or claims that result. We are not able to defend a claim on your behalf about work we did not make.

3. Rights — what you keep, what you give us

You keep the copyright. Your template stays yours.

You give us a non-exclusive right to display, market and sell it, and to deliver it to buyers under the licences described below. Non-exclusive means you are free to sell the same template elsewhere as well.

We may produce previews, thumbnails, watermarked images and promotional material from your template so that it can be shown and advertised.

You set both prices when you upload. There are two boxes — single-use and commercial — and whatever you type is what buyers are charged. You know what your work is worth better than a formula does.

Leave the commercial box empty and we set it automatically at 1.5 times the single-use price, rounded up to a whole dollar — so a $5 template gets an $8 commercial licence. You can change either price later.

You earn on both, on the same terms. Note that each price decides its own commission rate: a $4 single-use licence is charged 25%, while its $6 commercial licence is charged 20%.

Neither licence lets a buyer resell your template as a template, or claim it as their own design.

6. When and how you get paid

  • Earnings are held for 20 days from the moment the buyer pays.
  • After that they become available.
  • We send payment once your available balance reaches $50.

We pay through Whop

Whop is the payment provider that already handles every sale, so paying you through it costs nothing and converts nothing. The full amount you earned reaches you.

Opening a Whop account takes about two minutes and costs nothing — an email and a password. You do not need to verify anything to receive money from us.

Whop asks for identity details only when you withdraw to your own bank or wallet, and that part is between you and Whop. Every service that moves money does this — PayPal, Wise, Payoneer, your own bank. The difference here is that it happens once you already have money waiting, not before you have earned anything.

Why we are direct about this. Every other route loses money on the way to you. A bank transfer means your dollars are converted to local currency, sent, and charged for twice. A crypto withdrawal costs 5% plus a fixed fee before it even leaves. On a $50 payment that can be several dollars of yours, gone, for no benefit to either of us.

If you genuinely cannot open a Whop account — some countries are not supported — tell us in your application and we will agree an alternative before approving you. We would rather solve that at the start than approve someone we cannot pay.

We ask for your payment details only when your first payment is due. We do not collect or store bank accounts or wallet addresses before then, because we do not need them.

The 20 days exist because a buyer can ask for a refund, and a card payment can be reversed by the buyer's bank even later than that. Paying out sooner would mean sometimes asking for money back, which is worse for everyone.

7. Refunds and chargebacks

A buyer may request a refund within 14 days only if they have not downloaded the files. Once a download happens, the sale is final — digital files cannot be returned.

If a sale is refunded or reversed, the earnings from it are cancelled. If it is reversed after we have already paid you, that amount is owed back and will normally be taken from your next payout.

8. Removing a template

You may ask us to stop selling a template at any time. We will take it off sale, usually within a few days. Buyers who already bought it keep their licence and their access — we cannot take a purchased file back, and it would not be fair to try.

We may also remove a template ourselves: if it breaks these terms, if there is a rights complaint, or if the quality is not what we can stand behind. You keep earnings already made on sales that are not disputed.

9. Ending the arrangement

Either of us can end this at any time, in writing. Earnings you have already made are still paid to you on the normal schedule. Templates already sold stay licensed to their buyers.

10. Things we do not promise

  • We do not promise any level of sales, or any sales at all.
  • We do not promise to accept a template, or to keep it on sale.
  • We may change prices, and we may run discounts or promotions. Your share is always calculated on what the sale actually brought in.

11. Changes to these terms

If we change anything that affects what you earn or when you are paid, we will tell you before it applies, and it will not apply to sales already made.
