What LazyDog Editor does

Six jobs it does that a plain converter does not.

1

Convert PDF to editable PowerPoint (PPTX)

Upload any PDF and get a real PowerPoint (.pptx) deck — each page becomes a slide you can actually edit: retype text, move shapes, restyle. Not a flat image.

2

Convert PNG & JPG images to editable PPTX

Drop a PNG or JPG and LazyDog turns it into an editable slide and hands back a PPTX — add headings and elements on top instead of a locked picture.

3

Make any PowerPoint open properly in Canva

Some PPTX files import into Canva broken. LazyDog rebuilds them into a Canva-ready form so your design lands intact and editable.

4

Rebuilds masters, charts, WordArt & SmartArt

Slide masters, native charts, WordArt and SmartArt often arrive missing or blank in Canva. LazyDog rewrites them into standard shapes and text Canva can show and edit.

5

Trims off-slide "bleed" & recovers flattened designs

It cuts objects hanging off the slide edge and reconstructs flattened image-slides into editable elements — clean output, nothing spilling over, nothing locked.

6

AI — describe it, we build it

Ask the built-in AI to generate a design or a whole presentation from your text, drawing on LazyDog's own template library. Your co-designer, inside the editor.

FAQ

Can I convert a PDF to an editable PowerPoint?

Yes — upload the PDF and LazyDog rebuilds it as an editable PPTX, one slide per page, with real editable text and shapes.

Can I turn a PNG or JPG into slides?

Yes — drop the image in and it becomes an editable slide you can build on, exported as PPTX.

Why won't my PowerPoint open properly in Canva?

Canva doesn't fully support some PowerPoint features — masters, charts, SmartArt, WordArt, 3D and pattern fills can arrive missing or flattened. LazyDog rebuilds those into a form Canva accepts.

What does it cost?

Editing is free and always will be — type, restyle, move, resize and export as much as you like, with no watermark. File conversion and the AI tools run on tokens: every new account starts with 40 free ones, and plans begin at $19 a month.

Does it work without internet?

Editing does. Open the app with the wi-fi off and you can still create slides, type, restyle and rearrange. A connection is needed only for the parts that run on our servers — conversion, the AI tools, the template library and saved projects.
